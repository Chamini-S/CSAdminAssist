# Repeat Prescriptions

## How to order

You can order your repeat prescription:

- **Online**, through our website or the NHS App
- **By paper repeat slip**: hand it in or post it to us at reception

**We do not accept repeat prescription requests over the phone.**

## How long it takes

Please allow **2 to 3 working days** for your prescription to be ready. This does not include weekends or bank holidays.

## Collecting your prescription

Collect your prescription from your **nominated pharmacy**.
