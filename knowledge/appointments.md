# Appointments

## How to book

You can book an appointment **by phone** or **online**, through our website or the NHS App.

## Routine appointments

To book a routine GP appointment, contact us **from 8am** by phone or online.

We aim to respond to online queries **within 48 hours**.

## Urgent appointments

If your problem is urgent and you need to be seen today (it cannot wait), please **phone the practice**.

When the practice is closed, call **NHS 111**.
