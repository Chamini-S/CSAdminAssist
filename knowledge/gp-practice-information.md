# GP Practice Information

## When we're open

We are open **Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 6:30pm**. Our phone lines are open at the same times.

We are **closed at weekends and on bank holidays**.

## Staff training afternoons

We close on the **second Wednesday of each month, from 1:00pm to 6:30pm**, for staff training.

During this time, call **NHS 111** if you need urgent medical help, or **999** in an emergency.

## When we're closed

If you need urgent medical help when we're closed, call **NHS 111**. In an emergency, call **999**.

## How to contact us

You can contact us during our opening hours:

- **By phone:** 01632 960123
- **Online:** through our website or the NHS App

For non-urgent admin questions, you can also email reception@csdrclinic.example.com.

## Where to find us

CSDrClinic, 12 Doctor's Lane, Downtown, AN1 2CD

Website: www.csdrclinic.example.com

## Access and parking

Our entrance is step-free. We have a patient car park on site, including accessible (Blue Badge) bays.
